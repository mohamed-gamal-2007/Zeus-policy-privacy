Privacy Policy

How we collect, use, and protect information when you use ZeusEdu.

Last updated: November 9, 2026

This Privacy Policy explains how ZeusEdu ("ZeusEdu", "we", "us", or "our") handles information when you use our website, web application, and related services (together, the "Platform"). It applies to learners and other people who use the Platform.

Contact: For questions about this Policy or privacy requests, contact us at ziadmd207@gmail.com.

1. Information We Collect

The information we collect depends on how you use ZeusEdu. It may include:

  • Account information: your name, email address, login credentials, and account status.
  • Onboarding and discovery responses: answers you provide to AI-guided questions and assessments used to understand your interests, preferences, strengths, development areas, and career goals.
  • Learning and career profile: education, skills, experience, chosen career path, learning plan, roadmap, goals, progress, quiz responses, and learning activity.
  • CV and portfolio information: information and files you choose to provide or create, such as employment history, education, projects, skills, and portfolio materials.
  • Community information: posts, comments, messages, group memberships, and other content you choose to share in ZeusEdu communities, where available.
  • Technical information: device, browser, log, and diagnostic information, to the extent collected by our systems and hosting providers.
  • Support communications: information you provide when contacting us.

Please avoid submitting information that is not needed for your learning or career goals, or sensitive personal information, unless a feature clearly asks for it and you choose to provide it.

2. How We Use Information

  • Create and manage your account and provide access to the Platform.
  • Understand your interests, strengths, development areas, and career preferences based on your onboarding responses.
  • Recommend career paths, skills, learning goals, roadmaps, lessons, and learning content.
  • Provide AI-powered learning assistance, content generation, feedback, and assessment features.
  • Help you create or improve a CV and portfolio.
  • Identify and present job opportunities and match them with your profile, skills, and goals.
  • Operate communities and manage access to learning plans, accounts, and Platform features.
  • Respond to support requests, maintain security, prevent misuse, improve the Platform, and meet legal obligations.

3. AI-Powered Features

ZeusEdu uses AI-powered features to support learner discovery, career recommendations, personalized roadmaps, learning assistance, content, assessments, CV support, and skill or job matching.

When you use these features, information relevant to your request—such as prompts, onboarding answers, learning profile, skills, or text you submit—may be processed by third-party AI service providers. ZeusEdu currently uses Google Gemini and Groq for certain AI-powered features. The information sent depends on the feature you use. We aim to send only information reasonably needed to provide the requested feature. These providers process information under their own terms and privacy practices.

AI-generated recommendations may be incomplete or inaccurate and should be treated as guidance, not as a guarantee of educational, career, or employment outcomes. Review AI-generated CVs, content, and other outputs before using or sharing them.

4. How We Share Information

We do not sell your personal information. We may share information in these circumstances:

  • Service providers: providers that help us host, secure, operate, or support ZeusEdu, including relevant AI providers, where needed to deliver a feature.
  • ZeusEdu administrators: authorized administrators may access and manage learner account information and related profile, learning-plan, progress, and community information through the administration dashboard. This access is used to activate and manage accounts, assign or update learning plans, manage community membership, and provide platform support. Administrator access should be limited to authorized personnel and used for legitimate platform operations.
  • Private community members and administrators: ZeusEdu communities are private and access is managed by ZeusEdu administrators. Information and content you post or share in a community may be visible to members of that community and its authorized administrators.
  • Legal and safety reasons: where disclosure is required by law or reasonably necessary to protect users, ZeusEdu, or others.
  • Business changes: in connection with a merger, acquisition, or transfer of assets, subject to appropriate safeguards.

Creating an account does not, by itself, make your CV or profile public. Review the visibility and sharing settings of a feature before posting or sharing information.

5. Jobs and Third-Party Websites

ZeusEdu may display or recommend job opportunities from external sources. If you follow a link to a third-party job site or submit an application there, that third party handles your information under its own privacy policy and terms. Review those policies before submitting an application. ZeusEdu does not guarantee that a listed job is available or that an application will be successful.

6. Data Retention

We retain information for as long as reasonably necessary to provide the Platform, maintain your account and learning history, meet legal obligations, resolve disputes, and enforce our agreements. Retention periods may vary depending on the type of information and why it was collected. When information is no longer needed, we will take steps to delete it or de-identify it, subject to applicable legal and operational requirements.

7. Your Choices and Account Deletion

You may contact us to request access to, correction of, or deletion of personal information associated with your account, subject to applicable law and legitimate retention requirements.

To request deletion, email ziadmd207@gmail.com with the subject "ZeusEdu Account Deletion Request" and include the email address associated with your account. You may also submit a deletion request to the ZeusEdu administration team through an available in-app support or account-request option, if provided. We may ask for reasonable information to verify your request. We will review and process valid requests within a reasonable period, subject to applicable law.

Deleting an account may remove access to your profile, learning progress, roadmap, and community features. Some information may be retained where required by law or for legitimate security, dispute-resolution, or record-keeping purposes.

8. Security

We do not currently use third-party analytics services to track user activity for analytics purposes. Our hosting, security, or infrastructure providers may still process limited technical logs or diagnostic information needed to operate and secure the Platform.

We use reasonable technical and organizational measures designed to protect information against unauthorized access, loss, misuse, or alteration. No website, application, or electronic storage method is completely secure, so we cannot guarantee absolute security.

9. Children's Privacy

ZeusEdu may be used by people in different age groups. If you are a child under the age at which you can legally provide consent to the processing of your personal information in your country, you should use ZeusEdu only with the involvement and permission of a parent or legal guardian where required by law. Parents or guardians who believe a child has provided personal information without appropriate permission may contact us at ziadmd207@gmail.com to request review and, where appropriate, deletion.

Our age-related practices and the features available to younger users will be handled in accordance with applicable law and any platform-specific requirements. If ZeusEdu is offered to children as a target audience, additional child-safety, consent, content, and technical requirements may apply.

10. International Data Processing

Our service providers may process information in countries other than the country where you live. Where this occurs, we take steps intended to ensure that information receives appropriate protection in accordance with applicable law.
